## Brand & Style
This design system marries the tactile intimacy of high-end editorial fashion with the predictive precision of artificial intelligence. It balances high-fashion sophistication—luxurious negative space, high-contrast serif headlines, warm artisanal tones—against sharp, intelligent computational utility. 

The emotional objective is discovery without fatigue: curating an elite boutique environment where recommendations feel intuitive, bespoke, and human. The design aesthetic is Modern Editorial Luxe with Tactile Accents. Interfaces rely on luminous, warm-beige canvas layers, razor-sharp crisp white card platforms, and intense, authoritative berry-magenta highlights that anchor high-intent actions and algorithmic validation.

## Colors
The palette is structured to maintain high contrast while avoiding the sterile chill of purely achromatic software:

- **Primary (`#B81D5B` - Berry Magenta):** The signature vibrant accent. Used for critical forward-progress elements, high-confidence AI match indicators, primary CTAs, and interactive focus states. Hover states shift deeper to `#961347`, while active elements use light berry tint washes (`rgba(184, 29, 91, 0.08)`).
- **Secondary (`#1E1E24` - Deep Warm Charcoal):** The anchor for foundational content, primary typography, navigational structural frames, and definitive editorial statements.
- **Tertiary (`#0F9D58` - Precision Emerald):** Reserved strictly for algorithmic validation, high-affinity compatibility badges (e.g., "99% Fit Certainty"), and positive stock/inventory statuses.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Ecru & `#F4F1EA` - Muted Linen):** The warm, organic foundational background. Sets an editorial tone that softens optical fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used exclusively for product cards, flyout menus, search modals, and active cart containers to achieve punchy contrast against the ecru background.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#EAE5DC`):** Organic, low-contrast separator lines designed to provide micro-structure without boxing in layout breathing room.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy is bifurcated between high-drama editorial storytelling and rigorous analytical clarity:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved strictly for hero moments, collection titles, personalized style edits, and large section headers. It injects couture personality into the cold nature of recommendation algorithms.
- **Product Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Anchors all operational commerce surfaces: product names, prices, fit parameters, metadata, specifications, and button labels. It ensures legibility at micro sizes (e.g., swatch counters, sub-labels) and structural cohesion across high-density product grids.
- **Styling Rules:** Editorial headers must never be set in all-caps. Numerical data, especially price and AI match percentages, should always leverage tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate visual jitter during sorting or filtering updates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (max-width `1440px`), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Grid Rhythms:** Product listings use an asymmetrical editorial cadence: every 5th or 7th slot in standard 3- or 4-column product grids can expand to span 2 columns, serving as an "AI Lookbook Feature" or curated outfit composite.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Section transitions leverage ample negative space (`space-2xl` to `margin-xl`) to preserve an unhurried, luxury shopping tempo.
- **Mobile Adjustments:** Page margins collapse from `margin-lg` (64px) down to `margin-sm` (16px). Product grids shift to a dense 2-column format with `gutter-sm` (16px), prioritizing full-bleed touch targets and vertical rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is built using warm, tinted ambient drop-shadows combined with hairline borders rather than generic, heavy gray drops:

- **Surface Ground (`Level 0`):** `#FAF8F5` base canvas. Zero elevation.
- **Card Tier (`Level 1`):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle physical border (`1px solid #EAE5DC`) and an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Card Hover & Flyouts (`Level 2`):** Surfaces elevate dynamically on pointer focus: border transitions to `rgba(184, 29, 91, 0.2)` while shadow intensifies to `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(184, 29, 91, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Controls & Modals (`Level 3`):** The floating AI Stylist Assistant widget, bottom sheets, and full checkout drawers utilize: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.16)`.
- **Glass Accents:** Sticky headers, filter bars, and quick-filter pills utilize backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over a translucent surface (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)`), allowing product photography to subtly bleed through during scroll.

## Shapes
The shape language implements a modern, tailored contour system (`roundedness: 2`). Standard controls, text fields, and structural product containers carry an 8px (`0.5rem`) base corner radius, balancing structure with soft tactility.

- **Pill Exceptions:** High-salience communicative micro-elements—such as AI Match badges, seasonal promotional chips, size selector chips, and search queries—break strict bounding boxes with fully rounded capsule ends (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Interactive Buttons:** Primary CTAs carry an intentional, elegant 8px curve matching cards, avoiding exaggerated toy-like bubbles while maintaining a warm touch profile.
- **Media Corners:** Product thumbnails mirror card edges precisely with 8px clipping radii to eliminate misaligned corner gaps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#B81D5B` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), 8px border radius, zero border. Height: 48px desktop / 52px mobile. Micro-hover transition shifts fill to `#961347`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#1E1E24`, `#1E1E24` text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Icon / Wishlist Button:** Circular (40px x 40px) `#FFFFFF` housing floating at the top-right of image frames. 1px border (`#EAE5DC`), ambient Level 1 shadow. Icon is `#1E1E24`; active state animates a heart burst into filled `#B81D5B`.

### Product Cards
- Self-contained crisp white containers (`#FFFFFF`) framed by an 8px radius and hairline border (`#EAE5DC`).
- Image container holds a mandatory 3:4 aspect ratio with subtle zoom on card hover (`scale(1.03)` with 400ms ease-out).
- Card encapsulates: top-left floating badges, top-right heart action, product brand (`label-sm`, uppercase, `#6B6B76`), title (`title-md`, `#1E1E24`), price row (current price in `price-display`, strikethrough original price in `#8C8C96`), and rating display.
- Subtle quick-add button slides up from the card bottom on desktop cursor entrance.

### AI Match Badges & Chips
- **AI Match Pill:** Height 24px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), padding: `2px 10px`. Gradient fill option (`linear-gradient(135deg, #B81D5B 0%, #D81B60 100%)`) or emerald tint (`rgba(15, 157, 86, 0.1)` with `#0B7340` text) depending on the affinity threshold. Displays sparkle glyph (`✦`) alongside match percentage (`98% Match`).
- **Filter / Attribute Chips:** 32px height, pill-shaped, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border (`#EAE5DC`), text `#1E1E24`. Selected state: `#1E1E24` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Search & Fields:** 48px height, 8px radius, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border in `#D4CEC3`. Typographic scale `body-md`. Focus state transitions border to `#B81D5B` with an optical glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 29, 91, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px x 20px dimensions. Unchecked: 1.5px border `#8C8C96`. Checked: solid `#B81D5B` fill with pure white custom vector marks.

### Floating AI Stylist Assistant Widget
- Suspended at bottom-right viewport (desktop: 32px offset, mobile: 16px offset).
- Compact collapsed pill (48px height) featuring pulsating aura ring, iridescent sparkle icon, and "Style Assistant" copy.
- Expands to a 380px x 560px conversational canvas styled with a glassmorphic `#FFFFFF` panel (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), displaying personalized outfit recommendations, color pairing advice, and conversational outfit prompts.
